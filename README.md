# Qubic Church

> An open research log on the Anna Matrix, the Anna bot and Qubic's Aigarth project.

## About

Qubic Church documents research on the Anna Matrix, the answers of the Anna bot of Qubic's Aigarth project, and on related Qubic topics. Results are recomputed from public data, tests are fixed before they run, and every quote carries its source. Claims that did not hold up are listed openly on the corrections page of the site.

## Research areas

- **Anna Matrix:** where the table comes from, its mirror symmetry, and its relation to the public Aigarth unit.
- **Anna programs and Qubic mining:** how Anna 0.22 works and how it relates to Qubic's mining algorithm BPP9000.
- **Primary sources:** Come-from-Beyond's public statements, each with its message ID.
- **Community assets:** what is on chain for GENESIS, EXODUS and AIGARTH.
- **Interactive labs:** a matrix explorer, comparisons with trained Aigarth units, and emergence experiments.
