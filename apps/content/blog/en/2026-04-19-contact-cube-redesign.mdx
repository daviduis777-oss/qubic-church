## What changed

The **[Contact Cube](/evidence)** (Evidence Vault tab) has been comprehensively redesigned to match the quality of the Emergence Lab and Spectral tabs.

## Visual upgrades

- **Post-processing**: Bloom + Vignette effects on desktop for a dramatically richer scene. Mobile keeps the lean render for performance.
- **Gold wireframe glow**: Cube edges emit a soft gold highlight when folded, picked up by the bloom pass.
- **Subtler star field**: 1500 stars at depth 80 instead of 5000 shallow stars - more space, less noise.

## Interaction upgrades

- **Free camera**: Orbit, pan, and zoom freely. Camera presets now transition smoothly (lerped through OrbitControls) and release as soon as they reach their target.
- **Auto-tour**: Press `T` or click the tour button to cycle through all five camera presets at 6-second intervals. Any manual interaction cancels the tour.
- **Camera preset buttons**: All five presets (Default / Top / Front / Side / Isometric) are now visible as buttons. Previously only accessible via keys 1-5.
- **Color theme selector**: All five themes (Default, Fire, Ice, Matrix, Scientific) exposed as gradient swatches in the desktop panel and a cycle button on mobile.
- **Responsive controls**: A proper mobile bottom-bar layout replaces the hidden control drawer.

## Content integration

- **Genesis marker**: A new cyan-ringed marker at matrix position `[72, 72]` shows the PUSHBYTES_72 resonance from Chapter 26. Distinct from the gold `[22, 22]` primer marker.
- **Anomaly column color-coding**: The 68 anomaly cells are now colored by their column pair:
  - Gold: columns 22+105 (13 pairs)
  - Cyan: columns 97+30 (18 pairs)
  - Purple: columns 41+86 (2 pairs)
  - Emerald: columns 127+0 (1 pair)
- **Info modal expanded**: Now documents the period-4 attractor, the XOR triangle (100 XOR 127 = 27, and cyclic permutations), and the Genesis connection.

## Math correction

During the redesign we caught and fixed a math error that was present in earlier Contact Cube code: the Two's Complement identity formula was written as `sum = 0` instead of the correct `sum = -1`. The research documentation was always correct; only the 3D visualization code was wrong. All affected files are now consistent.

## Text scaling

Font sizes throughout the component were bumped to match the Emergence Lab conventions: all `8px`, `9px`, and `10px` text classes replaced with `10px` and `12px` minimums.

## What's next

Chapter 27 (Seven-Position Signature) is published alongside this release. The Contact Cube will gain a dedicated visualization of the seven asymmetry positions in a future update.
