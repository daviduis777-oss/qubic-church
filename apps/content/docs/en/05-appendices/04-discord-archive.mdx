## How to use this archive

- Quote only with a message ID, and prefer the full message over a fragment. Where we quote a fragment, the ID leads to the full text.
- A quote shows what CFB wrote, not that it is true. CFB often jokes, and some of his messages carry a troll emoji.
- For more verified quotes, see [chapter 15](/docs/03-results/15-discord-archive).
