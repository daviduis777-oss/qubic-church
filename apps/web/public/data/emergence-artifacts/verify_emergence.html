Why this works (the whole point): a machine that just memorized the 16 answers would be wrong on the 17th number it met. Training on the 16 smallest sums instead forces it to discover the actual rule for adding — sum = a XOR b XOR carry, carry-out = the majority of the three — because every one of the 8 situations shows up in those 16 sums. Once it has the real rule, the same little rule applied at every digit (carrying along) adds numbers of any size, forever. That is the difference between memorizing and understanding.
