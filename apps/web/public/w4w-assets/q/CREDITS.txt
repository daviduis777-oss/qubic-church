Models and textures in this folder are public domain (CC0 1.0).
- Quaternius (https://quaternius.com): animals, monsters, obelisk, campfire, chest, ruins, crystals and other props,
  from the per-model GLBs Quaternius published on Poly Pizza (as packaged in @netforge/asset-library-files);
  n/: pines, dead trees and rocks from the Stylized Nature MegaKit, textures resized to 512 px.
  Animations trimmed to the ones the game uses; monsters are recoloured as silhouettes in the game.
- ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com): tex/snow_* from Snow006, resized to 512 px.
