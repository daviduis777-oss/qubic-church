Models and textures in this folder are public domain (CC0 1.0).
- Quaternius (https://quaternius.com): animals, monsters, obelisk, campfire, chest, ruins, crystals and other props,
  from the per-model GLBs Quaternius published on Poly Pizza (as packaged in @netforge/asset-library-files);
  n/: pines, dead trees and rocks from the Stylized Nature MegaKit, textures resized to 512 px.
  Animations trimmed to the ones the game uses; monsters are recoloured as silhouettes in the game.
- ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com): tex/snow_* from Snow006, resized to 512 px.
- h/tallman.glb, h/crawler.glb: generated for W4W with Meshy (meshy.ai) from Qubic Church concept art; crawler hand-rigged in Blender.
- h/saint.glb (the Wailer), h/harbinger.glb (the Mourner): Meshy community models "The Hollow Saint" and
  "Harbinger of Decay", downloaded by Qubic Church from the Meshy community; decimated, re-rigged and repacked for W4W.
- h/shroud.glb: built for W4W from code in Blender 5.2 (own work), no longer used in the game.
- tex/tex_rock.jpg, tex/tex_ice.jpg: generated for W4W (own work). hero.glb: KayKit Rogue (CC0) with a recoloured cloak.
