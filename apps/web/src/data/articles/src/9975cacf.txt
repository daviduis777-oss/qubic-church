The one-paragraph version:
17 versions of Anna Aigarth in one week. 
That is how many the engineer Come-from-Beyond @c___f___b shipped in seven days. Every version builds on the one before it and prepares the launch of the first product of the Aigarth experiment: "BPP 9000", Bitcoin Price Predictor 9000. 
This product will be the brightest demonstration of a body of work that has been in the making, one way or another, for almost 25 years. And that is exactly why it is time to get ready for the mining marathon: the next epoch is not just another epoch. 
This post is the "why". The "what" comes next.
Part 1 - What actually happened this week
On 20 September at 17:35 CfB pinned this in price-discussion:
"In the following weeks we are launching BTC price predictor on real-time data. The result of its work will be either UP or DOWN each hour for BTC price. It's a free version, so it won't be giving more info (like magnitude of UP/DOWN swings). To gauge efficiency of this information we need to form a group of people who will be trading BTC taking the predictions into account. In this experiment every participant must start with same amount, $100. If you volunteer for the experiment, please, prepare those $100 and be ready to make history." - CfB, 20 Sep 2026
And right away we moved on to polishing new versions of Anna. A 28-day window. Noise switched on. A bug fix. Real BTC prices. 64 neurons, then 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384. Threshold 224, then 168. Then a year of data instead of a month. Then synthetic data without noise, and with noise.
This is kinda a lab notebook 
Part 2 - What the numbers said
Three results matter:
"So, what have we got: Using 67% accuracy on backtest data for progressing to the next hour we get 58% accuracy on non-seen data with noise. This is a promising result." - CfB
On real BTC price history, 25 September, 16:03:
"So, it's not 67%, just 64%, ok" - CfB
And the goal, stated for anyone who missed it:
"We don't know how many neurons is needed to predict BTC price with 67%+ accuracy, but we are doubling them in each new version." - CfB, 23 Sep 2026
"Our current goal is to find such number of neurons that accuracy doesn't approach 50%. That number may be millions depending on number of factors affecting BTC price." - CfB, 22 Sep 2026
And further:
"Anna does work on data without noise, she efficiently determines pattern. Noise makes it harder to do, too much noise would make it impossible. We working with synthetic data with noise now analyzing how ANN size helps to look through noise." - CfB, 26 Sep 2026
Part 3 - The wall at 4049 and what it taught
On 26 September every machine in the channel hit the same score: 4049. Different people, different hardware, different network populations, one number. CfB stopped the race for it:
"Guys, we need to do an extra task before finishing current, need to research that phase shift at 4049" - CfB, 26 Sep 2026, 13:32
"Looks like that 4049 is quality of data, not ANN" - 13:48
"This 4049 is amazingly consistent... and across ANNs with different population" - 14:37
The answer: "pre-grokking."
It is the same lesson as his post about Anna 0.10.32 and the genie: the model does exactly what the data allows.

Part 5 - What the miners will actually run
